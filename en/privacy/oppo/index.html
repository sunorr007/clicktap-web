Privacy Policy

2026-10-12 · This version of the policy covers the app distributed via OPPO Software Store.

Effective date: 2026-10-12 · Last updated: 2026-10-12

This policy explains what clicktap collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies only to the clicktap Android application (the App), provided by Beijing Fenghong Technology Co., Ltd..

1. Information We Collect

1.1 In the App

The App has no account system; apart from a task package you choose to share, it does not upload your task data. The OPPO build integrates Umeng analytics and crash-reporting SDKs (see section 5); there is no advertising SDK. Before first use, the App asks for your consent to this collection; nothing is collected until you consent.

  • What you record stays on your device. Tasks, action lists, loop structures, auto-start settings, execution logs, imported rule subscriptions and exported backups are all stored in the App's private storage on your phone.
  • Accessibility data is read, not transmitted. To perform the actions you recorded, the App reads accessibility node information — control text, control identifiers, content descriptions and coordinates — from the app you are automating. That information is used at that moment to find the right control and is not sent anywhere. What the App does store locally is what your task needs in order to work later: the target you chose for each step, such as a control ID, a piece of text, or a screen position.
  • Screenshots. If a step uses text recognition, the App asks Android for a screenshot to recognise text in the region you framed. Screenshots are processed in memory for that step. They are not saved to your gallery and not uploaded.
  • Notification text. If you enable a "Message" trigger, the App reads the title and body of notifications from the apps you selected, in order to check your keywords.
  • Installed apps. The App lists installed apps so you can pick a target. The list is read on your device and is not exported in your backups.
  • Sharing a task package (optional). When you choose to share a task package to the Playbook, you sign in with a third-party account first and the file is uploaded to our server. If you never share anything, nothing is uploaded.
  • Device identifier hash (trial and membership). After you consent to this policy, the App reads your device's ANDROID ID and converts it, through a one-way hash, into a random-looking string. The hash is used to record trial eligibility, attach the membership to you, and restore your purchase after you switch devices. It cannot be reversed back to the ANDROID ID; it is sent only to our own server and is never shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Information

  • To record trial eligibility, attach the membership to you, and restore your purchase after you switch devices.
  • To carry out the purchase, verification and restoration of the membership.
  • To measure feature usage, activity and retention, and to collect crash and freeze logs, so we can judge what needs improving and fix crashes.
  • To fetch a rule file when you choose to import rules from a URL.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We do not use your activity to train machine-learning models.

3. Permissions The App Requests

  • Accessibility service — required. It is what lets the App perform clicks and gestures, and read the controls needed to find them. Without it, replay cannot work.
  • Display over other apps — required for the floating recorder bar and the capture layer used while recording.
  • Notification access — optional. Used only by "Message" triggers, to read notification text from apps you select.
  • Foreground service (auto-start watchdog) — optional. The App runs this foreground service only after you create and enable an "auto-start" task (one that runs when you enter a chosen app), so it can reliably detect that moment in the background. You can check its status any time in Settings, under "Keep alive" (the "Background watchdog" card), and it stops by itself once all such tasks are removed. In all other cases the App runs no foreground service.
  • Alarms & reminders, and full-screen notifications — optional. Used so scheduled runs fire on time and so a locked-screen reminder can wake the display.
  • Battery optimisation exemption — optional, to reduce the chance that Android stops a run in the background.
  • Internet — used by the third-party services integrated in this build (see section 5), and when you choose to import rules from a URL or share a task package.

Every permission is explained in the App before you grant it, and each one can be revoked in Android settings at any time.

4. Storage, Security and Retention

  • App data stays in the App's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Your device identifier hash and your membership record are kept on our server, so that trial eligibility and the membership can be attributed and restored.
  • A task package you choose to share is kept on our server until you or we remove it.
  • We use HTTPS everywhere and restrict data access to the minimum each operation needs. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

These are the only third parties involved. We do not use advertising or tracking services.

  • Google ML Kit text recognition (App, optional feature). When a step uses text recognition, the App uses Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition models, in the Latin and Chinese variants. The models are bundled with the App and recognition runs on your device; text and images are not sent to Google. The SDK does send its own performance and usage metrics to Google (call durations, feature versions, error codes, device model and OS version); these do not include what you recognised. ML Kit is a Google product and is subject to Google's terms. If you never use a text-recognition step, this component is not used.
  • Umeng Mobile Analytics (U-App). Counts feature usage, activity and retention, to decide what needs improving. Collects device identifiers (Android ID, OAID, GUID, GAID) and network information (network type and connection state).
  • Umeng Application Performance Monitoring (U-APM). Collects crash and ANR (application-not-responding) logs, to locate and fix crashes and freezes. Collects crash stacks and exception information, device identifiers (Android ID, OAID, GUID), current process information and network information.
  • Alipay client SDK (app pay). Carries out the purchase and payment of the membership on the OPPO channel. The App never touches your Alipay account or payment details — our server signs the order string and hands it to the Alipay client. Involves transaction and order information, device information (device model, OS version, etc.) and network information. This channel has no channel account system, so the membership is attributed by the device identifier hash (a one-way hash of the ANDROID ID, see 1.1), and that hash is never transmitted to Alipay.

The in-app SDK list above matches the "About → Third-party SDK list" shown inside the App, which always reflects the build you have installed.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Request access, correction or deletion of the data we hold about you (your device identifier hash and your membership record) by writing tosupport@fenghongtech.cn. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Revoke permissions at any time. Every permission the App requests can be revoked in Android settings; the corresponding feature stops working once revoked.
  • If you are in a jurisdiction with specific data-protection rights, those rights apply to the data described above, and you may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us.

8. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer for international transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above always reflects the current version, and material changes will be noted in the App.
