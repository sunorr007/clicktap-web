1. What the App Does

The App uses Android's accessibility capability to record the actions you perform astasks, and then execute them by replay — manually, or automatically on a schedule, on receiving a chosen message, or when a chosen app comes to the foreground.

The App is a tool for automating your own device. It does not modify other apps and does not bypass their security mechanisms.
