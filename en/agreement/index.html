1. Description of the Service

clicktap records the actions you perform in other applications, using Android's accessibility capabilities, and replays them as a Task — either when you ask it to, or automatically on a schedule, on a matching notification, or when a chosen app comes to the foreground. The Site publishes documentation and lets signed-in users publish task packages to the Playbook so that others can download and import them.

The App is a tool for automating your own device. It does not modify other applications and does not bypass their security.
